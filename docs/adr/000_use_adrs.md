## Context and Problem Statement
<!-- 
What is the problem we are trying to solve? 
Why do we need to make a decision?
-->
Every codebase has a multitude of architectural inconsistencies and weird code constructs.  It can be difficult to understand or comprehend why a module is designed or behaves in a certain way. 


## Decision
We shall use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) like this one from now on to document our development, because it will make understanding the codebase way easier and will be a great aid in creating better code.
<!-- optional -->
## Consequences
<!-- Every decision has trade-offs. What does this mean for the future? -->
This may feel like unnecessary "homework". But using ADRs is standard practice in big codebases and can improve the shared understanding of the project. It is better to document little than to not document anything at all.
